# Case Study: FinPay Global (Fintech Sector)

## Challenge
FinPay Global needed to automate KYC document verification across 400,000 monthly transactions while meeting strict SOC2 and EU data residency laws.

## Solution
Deployed CloudScale AI Enterprise Tier in EU-Central with custom REST webhooks integrated into FinPay's existing PostgreSQL backend.

## Measured Results
- Reduced manual compliance review time by 78%.
- Cut customer onboarding time from 3 days to 4 minutes.
- Achieved 99.994% API uptime over 12 months.
