# Security, Privacy & Compliance Architecture

## Certifications
- **SOC2 Type II:** Audited annually by Deloitte. Reports available under NDA.
- **GDPR & CCPA:** Full data residency options in US-East (Virginia) and EU-Central (Frankfurt).
- **HIPAA:** Business Associate Agreements (BAA) are strictly restricted to Enterprise Tier customers using single-tenant VPC deployments.

## Data Retention & Zero-Training Guarantee
- Customer payloads are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
- CloudScale AI **never** trains foundation models on customer data. Zero-day retention is enabled by default for Enterprise accounts.
